Our story & heritage

About Owaisi Hospital

Established in 1996, Owaisi Hospital and Research Center stands as a beacon of hope and healing in the heart of Hyderabad, India. Named after its founder, Janab Sultan Salahuddin Owaisi Sahab, a prominent Indian politician and philanthropist, this institution has made significant contributions to the field of healthcare and medical research over the years. It is one-stop for pocket friendly medical services which includes out-patient and in-patient treatment, in-house diagnostic and radiological services. Our team is dedicated not just provide treatment but care too.

Beyond medicine lies humanity. Beyond treatment lies compassion. Every life deserves care, dignity and hope.

Established 1996

A legacy of healthcare and service.

Owaisi Hospital & Research Centre in Hyderabad was established in 1996 and named in honour of its founder, Janab Sultan Salahuddin Owaisi. From the beginning, its purpose has been to provide accessible, affordable outpatient, inpatient, diagnostic and radiology services.

Over the years the hospital has grown into a trusted name in the Kanchan Bagh area, serving families who rely on it for everyday care as well as emergencies.

  • Patient-focused care and support
  • Outpatient and inpatient services
  • Diagnostic and radiological services
  • A lasting commitment to community healthcare

OUR PURPOSE

Vision · Community · Research

Our Vision

HEALTH FOR ALL and RIGHT TO LIFE, by providing high end medical care by experts especially Owaisi Hospital was founded with the noble vision of providing accessible and affordable healthcare to all, Janab Sultan Salahuddin Owaisi Sahab, who was deeply committed to serving the underserved communities, wanted to create a world-class medical facility that would cater to the needs of the marginalized and disadvantaged sections of society.

Community outreach

Recognizing the importance of preventive healthcare and community well-being, Owaisi Hospital actively engages in community outreach programs. These initiatives include health camps, awareness campaigns, and educational workshops to promote healthy living practices and early disease detection. By extending its services to the wider community, the hospital seeks to make a positive impact on the region's overall health.

Research and Innovation

The "Research Center" aspect of Owaisi Hospital is an essential component of its mission. Over the years, the hospital has collaborated with leading medical researchers and institutions to advance the frontiers of medical knowledge. Pioneering research in various fields has contributed to the development of new treatments and medical practices, benefiting patients not only within the hospital's walls but also beyond.
